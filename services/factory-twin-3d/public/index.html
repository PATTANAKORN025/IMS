What each evidence tier means
▣ MEASURED
Read from the CAD or from a printed dimension. A number somebody wrote down.
▲ DERIVED
Computed from measurements, with the computation recorded.
◆ OBSERVED
Detected in a drawing. Real, but weaker than a stated dimension.
◇ SIMULATED
Invented for presentation. Nothing on this floor carries this tier any more.
? UNKNOWN / UNRESOLVED
Not established by any source. Stated as such, never filled in with a default.
✓ CONFIRMED
Backed by an authoritative record. Requires a real mapping document.
