Every cell is drawn somewhere. A cell with no CAD-backed position is never hidden — it is in its zone's drawer, marked spatially unresolved.

Two frames

FLOOR1_WORLD_M is the real floor plan, in metres, from the same geometry the floor's walls and columns are drawn from. 40 cells whose CAD instance is identified stand in it at that instance’s own measured position.

EAP_LAYOUT_FRAME is the reference layout’s own frame — schematic and anisotropic. The other 170 cells live there because nothing places them on the real floor; open a zone's drawer to see them.

The two are never merged and no transform takes one to the other. A set-level registration places a zone, not a machine.

Status

No authoritative mapping from a cell to an IMS machine exists, so every cell's status is UNKNOWN. The reference layout's colours are a snapshot of another system and are not drawn here.
