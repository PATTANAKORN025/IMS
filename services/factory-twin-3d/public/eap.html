Every cell is drawn. An unresolved position is marked, never hidden.

Two frames

FLOOR1_WORLD_M is the CAD floor plan, in metres. 40 cells whose CAD instance is identified stand in it at that instance’s own measured position.

EAP_LAYOUT_FRAME is the reference layout’s own frame — schematic and anisotropic. The other 170 cells live there because nothing places them on the real floor.

The two are never merged and no transform takes one to the other. A set-level registration places a zone, not a machine, so those zones appear on the floor plan as outlines with their machines left in the schematic.

Status

No authoritative mapping from a cell to an IMS machine exists, so every cell's status is UNKNOWN. The reference layout's colours are a snapshot of another system and are not drawn here.
